We are not trying to sell you more.
We are trying to make you buy better.

The listed price is not always the real cost of buying. Between middlemen, minimum order quantities, logistics fees and stacked margins, a product can end up costing far more than it should.

Centr works directly on that supply chain. We find the right manufacturers and suppliers, negotiate the buying terms and optimise volumes, so our clients get competitive conditions.

to the delivery.
